Module 5 is compressed to 2 units (one merged Foundation Lab plus the final Engagement Packet) and closes inside the December finals window. Everything is due Sunday — there is no mid-week deadline in this course.

All Due Times Are 11:59 p.m. US Central Time

Lab and Engagement Packet deadlines are 11:59 p.m. US Central Time (the home time zone of UT San Antonio) on their due date, no matter where you are located. Module exams open Sunday and close the following Tuesday at the same 11:59 p.m. US Central Time. Canvas enforces this cutoff automatically, so submit early to be safe.

How Module 5 Differs

Module 5 is the only module that runs as 2 units instead of 3. Lab 5.1 merges what would have been two separate Foundation Labs (vulnerability discovery and risk analysis) into a single longer lab. Lab 5.2 is the final Engagement Packet.

Lab 5.1 keeps the same Sunday deadline as every other Lab X.1 — only the scope is larger. There is no second Foundation Lab in Module 5; that time, plus Thanksgiving week off, becomes additional work time for the final Engagement Packet. Module 5 is also the one module whose exam is not decoupled ahead of the capstone: it opens Sun Dec 6 alongside Lab 5.2 and closes Tue Dec 8, so no one has to sit the exam and submit the packet on the same day.

Key Dates

First Day: Wed Aug 19  •  Last Day of Classes: Thu Dec 3  •  Finals: Dec 7–11  •  Final Grades Due: Mon Dec 14

University holidays and breaks: Mon Sep 7 (Labor Day) · Mon–Tue Oct 12–13 (Fall Break) · Nov 25–27 (Thanksgiving). Fall Break cancels two class days but nothing is due during it. Thanksgiving week (Nov 23–29) is a full no-deliverable week. No other holiday accommodations — every module unlocks in full at the start of its window, so work ahead if a break will affect you.

Registrar deadlines: Census Thu Sep 3 · Drop Deadline Fri Oct 23 · Withdrawal Deadline Tue Dec 1

Every Foundation Lab and Engagement Packet is due Sunday at 11:59 p.m. US Central Time. Module exams open Sunday of Week 2 and close Tuesday of Week 3. No late work is accepted — the one drop per category is the built-in accommodation.

Drop Policies

One drop per category, framed as built-in sick days:

• 1 of 10 Foundation Labs (Lab 1.1 is droppable like any other, but every lab after it needs your Kali VM — save your drop for one that doesn’t gate what follows)
• 1 of 5 Engagement Packets
• 1 of 5 Module Exams

Just like at work, sick days are not unlimited. Take more than one drop here and it comes out of your GPA in the form of forfeited grades.
