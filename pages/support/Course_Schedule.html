Spring 2027 · 16-Week Term · 5 Modules

Spring 2027 runs as a full 16-week term from Tue Jan 19 through Fri May 14. Modules 1–4 follow the same 3-week cadence: two Training Labs, a module exam, and an Engagement Packet. Spring Break (Mar 8–14) falls between Module 2 and Module 3, with nothing due. Module 5 is compressed to 2 units (one merged Training Lab plus the final Engagement Packet) and closes inside the May finals window. Every lab and Engagement Packet is due on a Sunday. Module exams open on a Sunday and close the Tuesday after.

All Due Times Are 11:59 p.m. US Central Time

Lab and Engagement Packet deadlines are 11:59 p.m. US Central Time (the home time zone of UT San Antonio) on their due date, no matter where you are located. Module exams open Sunday and close the following Tuesday at the same 11:59 p.m. US Central Time. Canvas enforces this cutoff automatically, so submit early to be safe.

How Module 5 Differs

Module 5 is the only module that runs as 2 units instead of 3. Lab 5.1 merges what would have been two separate Training Labs (vulnerability discovery and risk analysis) into a single longer lab. EP 5 is the final Engagement Packet.

Lab 5.1 keeps the same Sunday deadline as every other Lab X.1; only the scope is larger. There is no second Training Lab in Module 5, so Lab 5.1 and EP 5 each get a full working week. Module 5 is also the one module whose exam is not decoupled ahead of the capstone: it opens Sun May 9 alongside EP 5 and closes Tue May 11, so no one has to sit the exam and submit the packet on the same day.

Key Dates

First Day: Tue Jan 19  •  Last Day of Classes: Thu May 6  •  Finals: May 10–14  •  Final Grades Due: Mon May 17

University holidays and breaks: Mon Jan 18 (MLK Day, before classes start) · Mar 8–14 (Spring Break). Nothing is due during Spring Break. No other holiday accommodations: every module unlocks in full at the start of its window, so work ahead if a break or trip will affect you.

Registrar deadlines: Last Day to Add Wed Jan 27 · Census Wed Feb 3 · Drop Deadline Mon Mar 29 · Withdrawal Deadline Tue May 4

Every Training Lab and Engagement Packet is due Sunday at 11:59 p.m. US Central Time. Module exams open Sunday of Week 2 and close Tuesday of Week 3. No late work is accepted. The one drop per category is the built-in accommodation.

Drop Policies

One drop per category, framed as built-in sick days:

• 1 of 10 Training Labs (Lab 1.1 is droppable like any other, but every lab after it needs your Kali VM, so save your drop for one that doesn’t gate what follows)
• 1 of 5 Engagement Packets
• 1 of 5 Module Exams

Just like at work, sick days are not unlimited. Take more than one drop here and it comes out of your GPA in the form of forfeited grades.
