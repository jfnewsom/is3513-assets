Module 4 is where you go on offense, carefully. You'll learn how attackers target credentials, crack password hashes using industry-standard tools, and analyze what those results mean for an organization's security posture. Your client is Alamo Industries, assessed across two very different environments. Lab 4.1 cracks Windows (NTLM) hashes from Alamo's corporate office. Lab 4.2 moves to Linux (SHA-512) hashes from the plant floor. In Lab 4.3 you deliver one password security assessment to Alamo comparing the two.

⚠️ Offensive Techniques Module: These skills are for authorized security assessments only. Never use password cracking tools without explicit written permission. Everything here is on NEXUS-authorized client systems.

Client Context: Alamo Industries
Alamo Industries, a San Antonio aerospace and defense manufacturer pursuing CMMC Level 2, runs two very different environments: a Windows corporate office and a Linux plant floor. Elena Vasquez, the IT Manager, owns both and already knows they are not in the same shape. Bobby Alamo III, the CEO, is the executive sponsor. Your cracking results show them exactly where each environment stands and what to fix first.
